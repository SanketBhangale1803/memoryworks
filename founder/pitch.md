MemoryWorks: a memory layer for your engineering org and the AI agents working in it.
It connects GitHub, Slack, Google Drive, Notion and Teams and turns incidents, decisions, owners and dependencies into cited memories. People ask questions in one chat window and every answer shows its sources, or says it doesn't know.
Before an AI agent (Claude Code, Cursor, or your own via MCP or API) changes something, it gets a briefing: prior incidents, constraints, blast radius, and whether a person must approve. Agents report back what happened, so the next briefing is better.
Open source (MIT) and free to self-host with Docker. Managed cloud: $20 per engineer per month; the hosted version is in early preview.
